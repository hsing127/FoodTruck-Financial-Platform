### `App.js`

The main React component that handles the routing and rendering of different sections of the app, including:
- **Overview**: Describes the platform and its purpose.
- **Key Features**: Highlights the platform’s functionalities.
- **Motivation**: Explains the need for the platform.
- **Data Visualization**: Placeholder for future charts and graphs integration.
- **Manage Receipts**: Users can manage receipts after logging in.
- **Contact Us**: Form for user inquiries and feedback.
